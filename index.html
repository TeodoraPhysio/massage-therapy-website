Treatments

Every session is tailored on the day — I ask how you’re feeling, check what’s tight or sore, and adjust the pressure and techniques to suit.

Sports & deep tissue massage

My speciality. Firm, targeted work with stretching and joint mobilisation for training, tight muscles and desk-bound backs.

Recovery massage

Gentle, informed care after hip replacement, cosmetic surgery or cancer treatment — always in line with your medical team’s advice.

Hi, I’m Teodora

I trained in rehabilitation at Pleven Medical University and have been an HCPC-registered physiotherapist since 2012. For 15 years I’ve worked for myself in central London — first with home visits, then in my own practice on Wigmore Street, Welbeck Street and Berkeley Square, Mayfair.

My clients have included footballers, rugby and hockey players, marathon runners, and people recovering from surgery. What they value is massage that’s grounded in how muscles and joints really work.

I now live in Brentwood and bring that same care to clients here, in the comfort of their own homes.
